# Contest Retrospective — Monetrix

## Overview

This was one of my early public competitive audit experiences.

Main goals:
- improve protocol analysis skill
- practice adversarial reasoning
- develop structured audit workflow
- strengthen vulnerability validation process

---

## What Went Well

- Deep protocol exploration
- Better flow tracing
- Improved root-cause thinking
- More structured analysis process
- Stronger focus on invariants and assumptions

---

## Challenges Faced

- Distinguishing intended behavior from vulnerabilities
- Proving exploitability convincingly
- Avoiding overestimating impact
- Understanding nuanced accounting logic
- Separating architectural risk from valid contest findings

---

## Biggest Lesson

A finding is not only about:
"Something unusual happens."

It must clearly demonstrate:
- broken assumption
- security impact
- realistic exploitability
- protocol harm
- violation of intended guarantees

---

## Next Improvement Areas

Going forward, I want to improve:
- PoC construction
- exploit simulation
- protocol economics understanding
- precision in impact statements
- faster architecture mapping
- stronger validation before submission

---

## Final Thoughts

Regardless of final results, the experience was valuable.

Every audit:
- sharpens intuition
- improves pattern recognition
- exposes blind spots
- strengthens reasoning ability

The goal is consistent improvement over time.
